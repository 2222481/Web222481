1.13 Web 1.0

Meaning

Web 1.0 refers to the early stage of the World Wide Web, characterized by static web pages and limited user interaction.

Read only web

Characteristics

  1. Static web pages
  2. Read-only content
  3. One-way communication
  4. Limited multimedia
  5. Published by owners only

Role of Web 1.0

Web 1.0 played a crucial role in the early development of the internet, providing a platform for organizations and individuals to establish their online presence and share information with a limited audience.

Limitations of Web 1.0

Web 1.0 had several limitations, including:

  • Static content that could not be easily updated or modified.
  • Limited user interaction and engagement.
  • Dependence on the owner to update and maintain the website.

Technologies

Web 1.0 was built on technologies such as:

  • HTML (HyperText Markup Language)
  • HTTP (HyperText Transfer Protocol)
  • FTP (File Transfer Protocol)

Examples

  1. Personal Websites Individuals created static web pages to share information about themselves, their hobbies, or their interests.
  2. Company Websites Businesses established online presence with static websites to provide information about their products and services.
  3. Online Directories Websites that listed businesses, services, or other resources in a specific category or location.

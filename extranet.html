1.9 Extranet

Definition

A private network that extends an organization's intranet to provide controlled access to external users, such as partners, suppliers, or customers.

Role of Extranet

  • Secure Communication with External Partners
  • Collaboration with Suppliers and Contractors
  • Customer Support and Service

Used for

  1. Sharing information and resources with external stakeholders while maintaining security and control.
  2. Facilitating collaboration and communication between an organization and its partners, suppliers, or customers.
  3. Providing secure access to specific internal resources or applications for authorized external users.
